THE CONCEPT

Where student perspectives
meet on common ground.

Open Spectrum is a student-created festival concept developed by ALTA students at the ADYPU campus. Envisioned as a collaborative platform for creative unrest and multidisciplinary exchange, the proposed festival brings together sound, tactile design clinics, student discourse, and interactive installations.
